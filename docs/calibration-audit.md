## Findings

- The two boards, each at its printed size, give baselines of **47.10 mm and 46.84 mm** (0.5% apart). The value typed in as measured is 42 mm, +12% from them. Two independent prints agreeing makes a printing scale error unlikely (it would have to be the same on both); the 42 mm is the lens spacing of the CAD model, not of the optical centres. Measure the lens centres with a caliper, or a wall at a tape-measured distance (known_distance.py).
- `stereo_calib_charuco_42.npz` (what `config/pipeline.toml` uses) is the 25 mm board solved with 22.29 mm squares so that the baseline comes out at 42 mm: every depth from it is 0.897 times what the boards say.
